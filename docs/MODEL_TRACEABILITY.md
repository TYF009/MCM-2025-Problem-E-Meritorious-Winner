The three-species [Python example](../src/srcaed_simulator.py) uses `x⊙(r+Ax)`, independent Gaussian increments, one common noise amplitude and nonnegative clipping. No age axis, delays or impulses. It initializes `[10,2,0.5]` and runs to nominal `T=50` using `dt=0.01` and noise `0.03`; no seed is set. It computes `int(T/dt)−1` updates of size `dt` while its plotted grid includes `T`. These are **illustrative defaults**, with no paper unit mapping. The [MATLAB example](../src/srcmatlabaed_sensitivity_analysis.m) solves `Ax*=−r` and evaluates `diag(x*) A` for the deterministic drift, then runs `ode45`. This is local linearization at a feasible equilibrium when the linearization conditions hold. It does not establish stochastic/global Lyapunov stability and does not recalculate Fig. 15. The browser uses a separate normalized teaching model.

## 5D-OFE weights, decisions and forecasting

Fig. 11 ([p. 17](../paper/2521959.pdf#page=17)) maps EC to average temperature and water vapor; EM to pest and microorganism density; CP to crop energy and heavy metal content; PE to enduring sustainable policy (ESP) and cost-effectiveness (CE); SF to pH and organic matter density. [Table 2, p. 7](../paper/2521959.pdf#page=7) cites WA agriculture (markets), WMO (climate), WeatherWatch (crop advice), the Australian agriculture department (water) and FAOSTAT (pesticide use). No exact downloads, snapshots, joins, case data or full indicator matrix accompany the paper.
