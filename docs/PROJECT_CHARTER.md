# The Verdant Metamorphosis — Project Charter

**Status:** post-competition portfolio redevelopment, started November 2026.

## Mission
Use the original ICM 2025 Problem E team research to teach visitors how ecological dynamics, human intervention, uncertainty and economic constraints interact in decisions about sustainable agriculture.

## Portfolio story
- **2025:** Contest team investigated sustainability through mathematical modeling. Original submitted paper and award evidence are preserved unchanged.
- **2026+:** The author uses AI-assisted development and engineering methods to make research explorable, auditable, interactive, and clearer to audiences beyond the competition.

## Audiences
1. Technical hiring managers evaluating engineering, algorithms, systems thinking, communication.
2. Academic readers inspecting mathematics, assumptions and evidence.
3. General visitors experimenting with biodiversity and management trade-offs.

## Visitor journey
Problem → food-web relationships → model dynamics → experimental interventions → stability, economy and uncertainty → decision memo → source paper.

## Product deliverables
- **Research Story:** interactive, paper-cited narrative and team leader retrospective.
- **Figure Atlas:** source PDF page + exact figure number + crop + formula and chart explanation.
- **Living Farm:** time-series ecological dynamics and simulated ecosystem state.
- **Engineering Console:** equations, parameters, seeded solver, sensitivity and stability.
- **What-If Lab:** controlled pairwise management scenarios.
- **Decision Studio:** multi-objective optimization and evidence-linked farm brief.
- **AI Extensions:** optional, cited and reviewed natural language scenario construction and model challenges.

## Scientific integrity
Mark outputs **Original**, **Reconstructed**, **Illustrative**, or **Unverified**. The first website prototype is explicitly *illustrative*, uses synthetic/normalized state values and is NOT a reproduction of original paper figures. Real paper values only enter the validated implementation after figure/parameter audit.

## Source-of-truth policy
Official source: `problem/2025_ICM_Problem_E.pdf`.
Submitted research: `paper/2521959.pdf`.
Other images and scripts must be verified before attributed to contest implementation.

## Delivery gates
- M1: all relevant paper figures indexed and source-verified.
- M2: story citations/author retrospective approved.
- M3: solver, seed, units, tests and paper-to-model mapping.
- M4: at least three controlled, auditable strategy comparisons.
- M5: AI outputs constrained to evidence and solver interface.
- M6: accessible deployed website with CI, reviewed PRs and clear limitations.

## Team leadership caveat
The author can discuss his motivation and leadership philosophy in first person. Individual member roles, precise historical meetings and decisions must not be invented.
