# Sustainability research storyboard

Milestone 2 builds on the [paper audit](PAPER_AUDIT.md) and uses the [submitted paper](../paper/2521959.pdf) and [official Problem E prompt](../problem/2025_ICM_Problem_E.pdf) as primary sources. Each chapter starts with the visitor's question, gives the paper's account, and offers a technical disclosure. Interpretation View adds a current reading without changing any figure.

| Chapter | Visitor question and content | Evidence and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why sustainability? | Why use engineering models to study a working farm? Introduce resilient production, ecological processes and management choices. Connect the themes to SDGs 2, 12 and 15. | Paper pp. 3–4 and Fig. 1. SDGs are a thematic lens. |
| Decomposing the problem | Walk through problem, assumptions, methods, tests, evidence and limitations. | Prompt pp. 1–2. Paper pp. 3–6 and Fig. 2. Workflow describes the submitted analysis. |
| How the ecosystem evolves | Follow the food web, AED, succession and farm-cycle energy ranks. | Figs. 3, 5–7. Keep species counts separate from ordinal FPI and note missing series. |
| Changing the system | Compare native species return, pesticide withdrawal, bats and bees. | Figs. 8–10. Separate the prompt's herbicide question from the paper's broader withdrawal scenario. |
| Weighing farm trade-offs | Explain the 5D-OFE indicators, weighting, two objectives, forecast and sensitivity. | Figs. 11–15. Label reported scenarios and show missing inputs. |
| From models to the farmer letter | Summarize restoration, pest management and revenue diversification recommendations. | Paper p. 25 and its unnumbered radar chart. Baselines and forecast evidence are incomplete. |
| Team-lead retrospective | Use first person for the confirmed motivation and engineering mindset. Describe what I would change today in parameter capture, reproducibility, validation, sensitivity and AI-assisted interfaces. | 2026 reflection. Keep historical chronology and individual responsibilities out of the copy. |

Five expandable decision trails connect a question to the model or assumption, paper evidence, reported finding and open limitation. The chapter index links to each chapter and the team-lead reflection. Figure cards link directly to exact PDF pages. Preserve the figure atlas and the separate interactive model console and its disclaimer.
