# Agent instructions: The Verdant Metamorphosis

## Purpose
Transform the award-winning 2025 ICM Problem E research into an accessible, source-verified sustainability modeling portfolio. The author is a communication engineering graduate demonstrating mathematical modeling, responsible AI use, engineering execution, and team leadership.

## Non-negotiable provenance
- Preserve the original `paper/`, `problem/`, `figures/`, and historical `src/` assets. Do not overwrite original evidence.
- Classify every figure, numerical result, simulation, and conclusion as **Original 2025 research**, **Post-contest reconstruction**, **Illustrative demonstration**, or **Unverified provenance**.
- Do NOT fabricate original numerical values, paper figure numbers/pages, author contributions, team meetings, award rankings, datasets, or original source code.
- Verify quotations/claims against `paper/2521959.pdf` or `problem/2025_ICM_Problem_E.pdf` before publishing as paper-derived; if not verifiable, leave a clearly marked TODO.
- Do not silently alter mathematics to match publication charts. Document deviations, assumptions and limitations.
- Any AI-driven explanations must distinguish text grounded in the actual paper from new interpretation. Do not frame AI as scientifically validating a simulation.
- Avoid unsupported claims such as global Lyapunov stability from the eigenvalues of a local deterministic Jacobian.
- Never claim a simplified evolutionary optimizer is full NSGA-II. The current scripts are subject to re-verification.

## Engineering workflow
- Work in a feature branch and submit one focused PR per milestone; never force-push or modify main without review.
- Run tests/build and document commands/results in each PR; attach screenshots for visual changes.
- Keep site usable without paid AI APIs; no secrets checked into repository.
- Favor static GitHub Pages hosting, accessible mobile-first layout, semantic HTML, reduced-motion support, informative image alt text.
- Interactive results must be seeded and labeled with scenario assumptions/units; no simulated result should be mistaken for field measurements.
- The original contest submission must remain available as a first-class artifact.
- Team leadership sections should use supported retrospective first-person accounts and name uncertainty when exact chronology is unknown.
